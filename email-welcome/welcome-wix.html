Your first pause  ·  Sleep  ·  1 minute
Thirty minutes before bed, dim the lights and swap the screen for a paper book.
01Dim the lights in the room.
02Leave your phone to charge outside the bedroom.
03Open a paper book.
Why it works
After reading on a tablet before bed, people took about ten minutes longer to fall asleep, had less dream sleep and felt sleepier the next morning than after reading a printed book.
Chang et al. (Harvard Medical School), PNAS, 2015
