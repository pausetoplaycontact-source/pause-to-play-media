The Daily Pause

Breathe it into bloom

Breathe out slowly toward your phone. The watercolour opens only for a slow, soft breath. Hurrying won't work.

  • Hold the phone a hand's width from your lips.
  • Breathe in through your nose. Then let the breath out slowly, softly, as if fogging a window.
  • A quiet room works best.

Your microphone only listens for the sound of your breath, right here on your phone. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.

That was your pause.

Why it works. A long, slow out-breath engages the parasympathetic nervous system, the body's "rest and digest" side. In a 2023 Stanford study, five minutes a day of breathing with long exhales improved mood and lowered breathing rate more than mindfulness meditation alone (Balban et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2023).
